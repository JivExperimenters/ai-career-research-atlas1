## Deconstructed Real Mid-Level Job Descriptions

### 1. AI Agent Engineer (U.S. Remote) — [TRM Labs](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/trm-labs/828b60b2-ac8f-407d-92a0-8b794c8cf391)
* **Compensation & Structure**: **$200,000 – $275,000 base** + equity; distributed-first across the U.S. with hubs in New York, San Francisco, and Washington, D.C.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Architect modular agentic frameworks supporting tool use, context retrieval, episodic/semantic memory, and multi-step planning for financial and blockchain crime investigations.
  * Extend LLM infrastructure across OpenAI, Anthropic, and local models with semantic caches and vector databases.
  * Design auditable agent behaviors and evaluate performance across reasoning accuracy, millisecond-level latency, task success rate, and hallucination rates.
* **Key Transition Skill from Entry-Level**: Moving from single-prompt API calls to **observable, fault-tolerant multi-agent orchestration** with quantitative evaluation loops.

### 2. AI Engineer – Forward Deployed Engineering (AI FDE) — [Databricks](https://www.databricks.com/company/careers/professional-services-operations/ai-engineer--forward-deployed-engineering-ai-fde-8546367002)
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Build and roll out production GenAI applications—including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), multi-agent systems, Text2SQL, and custom fine-tuning—using HuggingFace, LangChain, DSPy, and PyTorch.
  * Partner with enterprise engineering teams on AWS, Azure, and GCP to move prototypes into governed, high-throughput production.

## Deconstructed Real Senior & Staff Job Descriptions

### 3. Senior AI Engineer (AI Automation & Product Integration) — [TRM Labs](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/trm-labs/d8498fe0-1f86-46e6-805a-626489ddd0cb) & [Blooming Health](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/blooming-health/a6d265bd-7e70-4225-9a8c-d49d552bf710)
* **Why This Matters**: Senior AI Engineers increasingly sit embedded inside cross-functional Product, Design, and Engineering pods—turning rough ideas into working agentic prototypes within hours and shipping reusable UI + agent components.
* **Key Technical Expectations**:
  * Real-time voice/conversational state management ([Blooming Health](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/blooming-health/a6d265bd-7e70-4225-9a8c-d49d552bf710), $170K–$230K remote).
  * Reusable component libraries and telemetry that prevent hallucination and silent failures in high-sensitivity environments.

### 4. Staff MLOps & Recommendation Systems Engineer — [Sequen AI](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/sequen-ai/cd7b81f3-9166-42e3-b3c6-9518677f3cb4) & [Quora](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/quora/1027c20b-ae70-4455-91be-73aac13610b6)
* **Compensation Band**: **$220,000 – $320,613 base** + equity (U.S. Remote).
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Design zero-downtime model CI/CD pipelines (canary releases, hot-swappable container rollouts, automated rollbacks) on Kubernetes.
  * Optimize GPU/CPU inference throughput, eliminate training-serving skew, and monitor real-time data distribution drift.
