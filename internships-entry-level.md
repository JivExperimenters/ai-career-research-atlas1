## Deconstructed Real Internship Examples

### 1. Multimodal & Embodied AI Intern — [Scout AI (Fury Team)](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/scout-ai/3b639e39-abd7-4a0c-9bea-e1cd0a72db99)
* **Mission Context**: Build Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models that allow operators to command fleets of autonomous robots through natural language.
* **What You Actually Do**:
  * Support the design, training, and evaluation of multimodal VLA models for perception, robotic actuation, and multi-step reasoning.
  * Build model experimentation pipelines utilizing synthetic data generation, simulation, and benchmarking environments.
  * Translate research papers in Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and Reinforcement Learning (RL) into testable Python/PyTorch code on real hardware.
* **Portfolio Proof That Gets You Hired**: A GitHub repo showing an end-to-end simulation or game environment where an AI agent makes sequential decisions under constraints, with clear evaluation metrics.

### 2. Large-Scale LLM Training Intern — [Cohere (Winter/Summer 2027)](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/cohere/36d1f52f-8270-4652-adf5-5303a0ff341b)
* **Location Advantage for NJIT Students**: Remote-friendly with a major office hub in **New York City** and a $500 home office setup stipend.
* **What You Actually Do**:
  * Train and improve autoregressive Transformer models on massive datasets using distributed training strategies.
  * Explore continual and active learning pipelines for streaming data.
  * Collaborate directly with product engineering teams to serve models faster and safer.
* **Portfolio Proof That Gets You Hired**: Hands-on benchmarks comparing fine-tuning or quantization techniques (e.g., LoRA/QLoRA, FastText/Transformer embeddings, or JAX/PyTorch latency profiling).

## Deconstructed Real Entry-Level (New Grad) Examples

### 3. Forward Deployed AI Engineer (New Grad) — [LiteLLM](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/litellm/769df1b5-70bb-40fe-b2e2-ef052eb3afa3) & [Applied Intuition](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/applied/289cead6-d731-4ca4-95e2-e83cee83c20c)
* **Why This Role Is Booming for New Grads**: "Forward Deployed Engineer" (FDE) has become the premier entry-level AI title in 2026–2027 ($150K–$210K at [LiteLLM](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/litellm/769df1b5-70bb-40fe-b2e2-ef052eb3afa3)). Instead of sitting behind a narrow ticket queue, New Grad FDEs sit at the intersection of **AI engineering, product architecture, and customer problem-solving**.
* **What You Actually Do**:
  * Diagnose root causes in production LLM gateways, API routing, rate limits, and RAG pipelines for enterprise customers.
  * Build custom integrations, full-stack dashboards, and deployment scripts in Python, TypeScript/React, or C++.
  * Translate ambiguous real-world user workflows into working software within days.
* **Portfolio Proof That Gets You Hired**: Shipping a complete, live AI-powered web app or developer tool with clean UI/UX, multi-model API routing, error handling, and real user feedback.
